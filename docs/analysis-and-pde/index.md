# Analysis and PDE

Where norms, operators, and regularity turn difficult geometry into useful estimates.

## Overview

Analysis keeps showing up behind the scenes. Sobolev norms measure smoothness, elliptic operators connect geometry with spectra, and PDE estimates turn qualitative ideas into quantitative control. These tools are especially useful in automorphic forms, spectral theory, and homogeneous dynamics.

## Topics I’m learning

- Sobolev spaces, norms, and embeddings
- Distributions and weak derivatives
- Elliptic operators and regularity
- Laplacians, eigenfunctions, and spectral theory
- Energy estimates and functional inequalities
- Smoothing, decay, and effective equidistribution
